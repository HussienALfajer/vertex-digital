## Summary
57 findings: no P0, 1 P1, 17 P2, 39 P3. 49 fixed, 1 partly fixed, 2 deferred, 5 moved to A10; one owner question answered and built (Q28), one settled with A10 (Q29). The money and ownership rules held: every ticket, message, attachment and notification query filters by its customer, the freeze and archive paths lock in one order, the Telegram webhook and its decisions are guarded, and every admin action writes its audit entry in its transaction. What failed was around them: work done before a refusal, the live stream's client, and the screens' stale and failed states.

What changed:
- **Uploads (A07-1):** a ticket or a reply sure to be refused (the open and daily limits, the hour's messages, the link, a ticket closed past 7 days, an archived customer) is refused before any image is re-encoded or stored, then checked again under the lock; a customer over every limit no longer writes files to disk.
- **Live stream (A07-2, A07-3, A07-30, A07-31, A07-32):** a signed-out visitor opens no stream (reproduced live: a 401 every 5 s from a game page with a pack chosen); a session that ended stops asking; a 4th tab no longer knocks the others off in a loop; the API reads no notification for a customer with no open stream and forgets quiet customers; nginx counts open streams in their own zone.
- **Support rules:** Q28, an admin reply on an archived customer's ticket is refused and the panel says why; ticket writes lock the customer, so an archive and a ticket write never interleave (A07-19); an opening's key replays only the same ticket (A07-23); a malformed id is a 404, not a server fault (A07-4).
- **Store screens:** a failed background read keeps the thread, the typed reply and the lists with a notice (A07-6); the newest answer wins (A07-7); the pages read again when the tab is seen (A07-9); a failed close says why in its dialog (A07-8); the ticket and reply keys survive a reload (A07-39); the picker's errors are proper field errors (A07-34); the bell and the center no longer prefetch (A07-35).
- **Panel screens:** the thread and queue read again (A07-10); the merchant-applications badge (A07-12); a confirmation before archiving or replacing a pinned note (A07-13); failed pickers with retry (A07-11, A07-14); dimmed stale rows (A07-15); not-found and stale states (A07-47); a warning when a per-deposit override exceeds the daily limit (A07-22); the Telegram test message's status updates (A07-43); 44 px targets (A07-51).
- **Telegram and email:** the ticket subject is sent as code, so a link in it is never clickable (A07-24); a deposit card waits out a 429 (A07-26); a flood of `/start` codes gets one reply per chat per 10 minutes and no longer takes the links' lock (A07-27); SMTP gives up in seconds (A07-29).
- **Database:** the history reads by entity type (A07-5); a page of tickets reads its links in two queries (A07-21); migration 0096 adds five indexes (A07-21, A07-28; A05-60's `telegram_messages` index among them); the limits upsert stamps its change (A07-25).
