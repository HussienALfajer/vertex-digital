## Summary
41 findings: no P0, 1 P1, 20 P2, 20 P3. 36 fixed, 2 partly fixed (test gaps), 3 deferred; one owner question (Q30), one decision taken under the owner's delegation (A09-20). By dimension: spec 9, backend 5, database 4, worker 1, frontend 9, UI 7, security and fraud 1, performance 3, tests 5, observability 1 (a finding counted once, under its first dimension).

The rules that matter held: every switch is enforced on the server under the switches lock on every write path (purchases, checkouts, panel orders, reservations, deposit creations), frozen customers are refused everywhere, movements, results and audit rows are append-only by trigger and grants, every money route is re-authenticated with an `Idempotency-Key` and a typed confirmation above $100, reports exclude test customers and equal the dashboard, and the export writes strings, never formulas. What failed was around them.

What changed:
- **Books (P1):** a wrong opening balance reversed after the place was used can be taken again; it used to leave the place's balance check skipped every night (A09-1). The USDT opening is proposed from the chain's balance (A09-2).
- **Money safety:** one incoming TXID can no longer be booked both as an unclaimed receipt and by a deposit (A09-3); a supplier's pause no longer holds every purchase and deposit while it reprices (A09-23); the nightly run no longer fails against a manual run started at 00:30 (A09-7).
- **Store:** the stop and frozen banners follow a stop, a reopen, a freeze or an unfreeze on the next page, and agree with the buy box (A09-22); the POS and bulk pages say purchases are stopped before a merchant fills a sale or spends a check (A09-24).
- **Reports:** cancelled reservations no longer count as finished orders (A09-11); the merchant breakdown counts orders placed as a merchant (A09-20); the supplier totals count a split order once (A09-17); the export formats every place in its currency, keeps manual deposits apart from the totals and dates itself in Damascus (A09-19); year-0 dates are refused, not a 500 (A09-18); three indexes for R6, R2 and the audit log (A09-12, A09-14, A09-16).
- **Panel:** the dashboard's stale note shows after failed reads and its profit is in cents as R1 (A09-27, A09-37); reconciliation says "clean" only after a night checked the books, re-enables "run now" when its 5 minutes end, refreshes the places when a run ends, explains an unknown order under its field and labels a failure (A09-28, A09-29, A09-32, A09-35, A09-38); report presets follow midnight and the export download is kept (A09-30, A09-34); the audit filter refuses a reversed range and its short ids tell records apart (A09-13, A09-40); a supplier pause is named as one and linked to its supplier (A09-41).
