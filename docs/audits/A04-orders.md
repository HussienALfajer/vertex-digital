## Summary
56 findings: no P0, 3 P1, 15 P2, 38 P3. 48 fixed (A04-21 after the owner's answer to Q24, in a PR of its own), 2 partly fixed (the E2E gaps, the rest deferred), 1 dropped (A04-23), 3 moved to A10, 2 deferred. The money core held up: one access decorator per route and the owner's filter on every customer read, the price read under the product lock with `PRICE_CHANGED`, one refund journal per order, the debit and the routing job in one transaction, unknown outcomes polled before any other route, idempotency keys and audit entries on every admin decision, codes encrypted and never logged.

What changed:
- **Customer:** the cart can no longer pay twice while a checkout is in flight, and a key rule change keeps any edited body under the first key (cart, buy box, every form); a removed cart line comes back with "تراجع"; the order page keeps the revealed codes when a refresh fails and never shows an older state over a newer one; the checkout list never mixes two queries; a failed balance read says so with a retry; the merchant terms sheet says nothing was paid; the print page's sign-in keeps the paper and content, and codes not yet delivered say so without a futile retry; the problem sheet keeps what was typed when the problem no longer applies; the alerts bell follows "تنبيهاتي"; each code's reveal button names its code.
- **Admin:** the refund dialog states the amount, the units and where the money goes (the customer's wallet or the owner's money); `/orders/new` confirms the payer, the pack and the total before paying, asks before leaving, retries failed reads, warns of a short balance or cap, and never shows the previous customer while the next loads; the live room's side sheet follows its order, the board says when it is stale; event reasons read in Arabic; the order dates are Damascus days.
- **Backend:** a held order left without an attempt is routed again by the sweep; an outcome's transaction picked by a deadlock runs again; the sweep's steps no longer stop each other; a fast webhook waits for the first send's answer; a poll sends nothing for an attempt closed meanwhile; a slow first send is not re-sent beside itself; a replayed purchase answers its order even at the rate limit; a proof serves only the order it was uploaded for; a checkout line reads routing once; player-check failures keep their cause; share images are kept 5 minutes; a malformed alert id is not found.
