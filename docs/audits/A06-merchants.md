## Scope

- **Store screens:** `/account/merchant` (`features/merchant/merchant-page.tsx`, the four-step `application-form.tsx`), the account page's `merchant-card.tsx` and the MT2 `terms-sheet.tsx` (opened by the card, the buy box, the cart, and now the POS and bulk pages), `/merchant-terms`; the merchant area `app/merchant/(area)/` (layout with `MerchantNav`): the dashboard, `pos`, `bulk`, `prices`, `price-list`, `statements` and `statements/[month]`, `alerts`, `receipt`; the print pages `app/merchant/(print)/price-list/print` and `statements/[month]/print`; the order page's `order-tools.tsx`, `/orders/[id]/print`, `/v/[code]`; `features/merchant-tools/*` (`merchant-state.tsx` `useMerchantRead`, `requests.ts`, `form.ts`, `messages.ar.json`).
- **Panel screens:** `/merchants/applications` and `/merchants/applications/$id` (approve, request information, reject, the photo), `/merchants` (search, list and level filters, revoke), `/merchants/levels` (add, edit, archive), `/settings/merchants`; the customer profile's merchant parts (grant, revoke, price list, branding and logo removal).
- **API:** `merchants` module (`/api/merchant/application` with its photo, withdraw and `/api/merchant/terms`; `/api/admin/merchant-applications`, `/api/admin/merchants`, `/api/admin/customers/:id/merchant` grant, revoke and logo, `/api/admin/merchant-settings`, `/api/admin/merchant-levels`), `merchant-tools` module (`/api/merchant/*`: dashboard, games, selling settings and prices, watches, alerts and settings, recent players, bulk check, receipt settings and logo, price list and its PNG, statements and their export; `PUT /api/orders/:id/sale`), `GET /api/verify/:code`; the purchase, checkout and on-behalf write paths for the terms and frozen checks.
- **Worker:** `merchants.expire-applications`, `merchants.level` and `merchants.level-schedule`, `merchants.watch-alerts` and `merchants.daily-digest`, `merchants.statement-emails`; the merchant emails and pushes.
- **Database:** `packages/db/src/merchants/*` (accounts, applications, levels, reads), `packages/db/src/merchant-tools/*` (alerts, counter, reads, sales, statements, watch alerts), `customers/state.ts` (archive blockers); schema `merchants.ts` and `merchant-tools.ts` with migrations 0080, 0081, 0083–0089.

## Summary
56 findings: no P0, 4 P1, 22 P2, 30 P3. 47 fixed, 4 partly fixed, 2 deferred, 3 moved to A10; two owner questions answered (Q26, Q27). The server held the money rules: every purchase, checkout and bulk check refuses a frozen merchant and missing terms, prices and quantities are re-read on the merchant's effective list, keys replay; what failed was the counter's front end around them.

What changed:
- **Money at the counter:** a POS sale and a bulk payment keep their `Idempotency-Key` in session storage, so confirming again after a lost answer (a sheet closed, a page reloaded) replays the first order instead of paying twice (A06-1 reproduced live before the fix: two delivered, debited orders for one sale; after it, one key and one order). A changed price shows the new total and is confirmed with it; a refusal for the terms opens the terms sheet; a frozen merchant sees the notice and nothing sends; "ادفع الكل" names its total and waits for a top-up while the balance is short.
- **Levels:** a refund counts against the month its order was paid in (Q26), so orders bought to fail at a month's end no longer keep the better list for the next month; accepting the terms locks in the order of a revocation and the level job (no deadlock).
- **Merchant tools:** the bulk check runs its player checks five at a time within 30 s; the dashboard and sale prices count only orders paid as a merchant; one digest an hour; statement reads limited; the monthly and hourly jobs no longer stop at one failing merchant; the price list image renders under one time budget; the logo upload keeps a saved WhatsApp.
- **Screens:** reads that land newest-only with the old data dimmed, in parallel; failures with a retry (the POS strip, the account card, the panel's pickers and the applicant's summary); 44 px targets and tappable switch labels; page titles with the store's name; the order page without the merchant catalog. Q27: codes print in the POS strip, games by 30-day units, the unit price per bulk line, a template with every pack, the price list printed and back with its options; 23 unused strings dropped and S21 amended for the layout kept.
- **Panel:** approval blocked for a frozen or archived applicant with the reason; the back arrow; typed list queries; focus on a refused reason; the level limit; the level's list named on the customer's pricing card.
- **Accounts:** an open application blocks archiving; photos only for an application the customer may send; the approval email names the price kind; the expiry job passes over a failing application; an answered information request leaves the customer's view.
