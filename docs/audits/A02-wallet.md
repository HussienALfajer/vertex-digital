## Summary
43 findings: no P0, 3 P1, 19 P2, 21 P3. 40 fixed, 3 P3 deferred (below). The three P1 are the money paths: the TXID of another customer's transfer, the editable manual deposit, approvals without a confirmation. One business question went to the owner (Q23). The ledger core held up: append-only and balanced journals, integer money, one credit per deposit, owner filters on every customer route, audit in the same transaction.

What changed:
- **Money and fraud:** a TXID pasted from someone else's exact transfer credits the deposit that asked for it and bounces the paster; a manual deposit of a recorded transfer cannot change its network or exceed what arrived; a USDT deposit waits for a late scanner before it expires; requotes and cancellations are limited per customer and at nginx.
- **Admin:** an approval asks once more, stating the amount and the customer; the U13 dialog shows the transfer's facts and the customer, read-only, with a way back; the money buttons state the amount or the rate; the queue says when it is out of date, a USDT deposit under verification follows the worker, a refused clearer-receipt request offers the rejection, the receipt and the audit trail recover from a failed load; back arrows point back.
- **Customer:** the wallet and "إيداعاتي" follow credits live without losing loaded pages; a failed background read never wipes a deposit page; late reads cannot undo a TXID or a cancel; the QR loads only where it is shown; receipt refusals speak of one image.

## Owner decisions
- **Q23** (`docs/open-questions.md`): the USDT reservation grace of a credited exact amount (rule U4, 7 days). Recommended 30 days. Unanswered: 7 days stays.

## Deferred
- **A02-28** (P3): the scanner never moves past a listed transfer it could not settle. Deliberate: moving on would drop that transfer from the scan for good; the stale alert and the network's delayed state already fire. A dedicated alert naming the transfer is recorded for A10.
- **A02-29** (P3): a database guard tying a credited USDT deposit to its bound transfer. Defence in depth only (one write path, `creditUsdtDeposit`; reconciliation C8 checks the journal); it rewrites a guard trigger on `deposits`, a migration with its own risk. Recorded for A10.
- **A02-30** (P3): an index for TXID lookups. V1 volumes are hundreds of rows; it needs a migration. Recorded for A10.
- From the agents' reports, not taken as findings: the deposit queue's email search keeps the first 100 matching customers (a broad prefix narrows by typing more); the rate change takes no `Idempotency-Key` (the spec asks for none, and a double submit appends an identical, audited row); `updated_at` from the app's clock beside `now()` in `usdtReserved` (cross-cutting, A10); TronGrid's indexing order (could not be confirmed); the panel-wide FormData `body as never` (A10); the receipt toolbar's 32 px buttons (the 44 px rule is the store's).
