## Summary
81 findings: no P0, 8 P1, 37 P2, 36 P3. 53 fixed (A05-8 with an owner question, Q25), 4 partly fixed, 15 deferred, 6 moved to A10, 3 dropped. The money core held up: integer and BigInt price math rounding up, prices never below cost, product locks in id order with no cycle against purchases, every route and rule change audited in its transaction with re-authentication where the specs ask, supplier keys encrypted and never answered or logged, webhooks verified in constant time within a replay window, unknown order outcomes never failed.

What changed:
- **Prices:** a review accepted or adjusted at the held price writes the new rule and cost, so purchases copy the right minimum and the next sync opens no review again; a held price is guarded by the minimum its row carries, as orders are; a sync no longer writes back a cost a charge or a quote recorded while it read; repricing many lists at once is written in chunks; one live manual route a product.
- **Supplier imports:** the game amount is suggested only for packs in the main unit, and a link proposes an existing pack by its amount only when its routes deliver the same unit (no more "660 WOW coins" on "660 UC").
- **Supplier jobs:** a lapsed FazerCards plan is no longer synced every 15 minutes nor shown as an outage; a failing sync is told again every 6 hours and the daily summary lists failing syncs and inactive plans; a WDGZone read of up to 15 minutes is no longer closed as abandoned while it reads; products left on a stale cost are not relocked every minute; one supplier's health failure no longer stops the others.
- **Adapters:** a WDGZone 410 and a FazerCards 404 count only in the supplier's own words; FazerCards' rate-limit header is read as the seconds left; a gift card's codes are read without its kind; a hex signature in capitals is accepted.
- **Panel:** archiving or switching off a route, unlinking or ignoring a category, and accepting reviews (one or many) ask first and state the price; the margin and matrix dialogs show their wait and failures inside the dialog; failed reads offer a retry throughout the group; the preview waits for a pause in typing; the matrix shows retail alone, its cells read their price to a screen reader and link to the list's rules; a fixed price below the floor says what the list will take; list refusals land on their field; the low-balance threshold says it was saved; amounts keep their order in Arabic sentences.
