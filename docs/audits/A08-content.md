## Scope
Mapped from the code.

- **Store screens:** the announcement bar on every page (`features/content/announcement.tsx`, `close-announcement.tsx`, `dismissal.ts` with its constant script; `packages/ui` `AnnouncementBar`), the home banners (`home-banners.tsx`, `banner-carousel.tsx`), `/faq` (`faq-page.tsx`), `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refund`, `/about` and `/merchant-terms` (`content-page.tsx`; `packages/ui` `RichText`), the footer links, the sign-up sentence and the support callout (`site-links.tsx`), `sitemap.ts`, the cached reads (`data.ts`, tag `content`, 60 s), `POST /_internal/revalidate`. Live activity left the store on 2026-10-19 (LA7); `packages/ui` `ActivityFeed` stays for the panel's preview.
- **Panel screens:** `/content/pages` (the five pages), `/content/pages/$page` (editor, preview at phone and desktop widths, `Ctrl+S`, publish, versions with view and restore, the stale dialog, the leave guard), `/content/faq` (sections and questions, move buttons and drag, archive and restore, the question dialog with preview, the publish bar and its preview), `/content/banners` (rows, enable switch, the "N من 5 مفعّلة" count, the banner dialog with the 2:1 upload and schedule), `/content/announcements` (rows, enable with the replace confirmation, the dialog with the bar's preview), `/content/activity` (the switch, the qualifying count, the preview); the accepted versions on the customer profile (TA3).
- **API:** public `GET /api/content/site`, `pages/:page`, `faq`, `activity` (`public, max-age=60`); admin `/api/admin/content/*`: pages (list, draft, version, save, publish, restore), FAQ (working copy, sections and questions create, edit, archive, restore, order, publish), banners (image upload, list, create, edit, enable, disable, archive, restore, order), announcements (the same), settings, the activity preview; every admin change queues `store.revalidate` through `StoreRevalidateInterceptor`. The sign-up's `acceptedVersionsNow` and the profile's `customerAcceptedVersions` (`packages/db/src/content/versions.ts`), the live activity read (`packages/db/src/content/activity.ts`).
- **Worker:** `store.revalidate` (expires `catalog` and `content`); no job of its own.
- **Database:** `content_page_drafts`, `content_page_versions` and `faq_publications` (append-only, migration 0048), `faq_sections`, `faq_items`, `banners`, `announcements` (one enabled, the link path and schedule checks), `content_settings`, `customers.terms_version_id` and `privacy_version_id` (guarded by `customers_accepted_versions`); migrations 0047, 0048, 0081 (`merchant_terms`).
- **Tests:** api `content.test.ts`; contracts `content.test.ts`; store unit `content.test.ts`; store E2E `content.spec.ts` (the content store on port 4004); admin E2E `content.spec.ts`, `forms.spec.ts` (editor tabs).

## Summary
47 findings: no P0, no P1, 15 P2, 32 P3. 39 fixed, 4 partly fixed, 1 deferred, 3 moved to A10. No owner question. The rules that matter held: content renders only through the parser (no raw HTML, internal links only, the database checking the link paths too), versions and publications are append-only, every change writes its audit entry in its transaction, live activity leaks nothing and the one-enabled announcement holds under concurrent enables. What failed was around them: destructive actions without a question, screens that dropped what they showed on a failed read, the carousel's first view, and the merchant terms' path.

What changed:
- **Panel, safety:** archiving a banner, an announcement, a FAQ section or question asks first and says what happens to the store (A08-5); a stale draft can be copied before the reload (A08-9); a stale FAQ publish closes its dialog and asks for a review (A08-13); an unchanged draft is not saved again and `Ctrl+S` waits under a dialog (A08-24).
- **Panel, states:** a failed read after the first keeps the screen and what is typed, with a notice and a retry (A08-6, A08-22); the lists read again when the tab is seen, as background requests (A08-23); the activity preview follows the switch and its note says the store shows no feed (A08-7, A08-8); a refused reorder says the list changed (A08-12).
- **Panel, detail:** the merchant terms at `/merchant-terms` (A08-16, `contentPagePath` in the contracts); link warnings on banner and announcement links (A08-15); a warning when an announcement is cut on a phone (A08-32); row names in every switch and button, focus kept by the move buttons, 44 px buttons (A08-11, A08-20, A08-21); the preview's update day in Damascus (A08-27); a test customer's versions (A08-28).
- **Store:** the carousel starts on the first banner in full, centred once it has a width, with 44 px dots and pauses that end (A08-34 to A08-37); no third line under a clamped announcement and the focus kept after closing it (A08-31, A08-38); the sitemap's `/merchant-terms` (A08-33); `/faq`'s title while unpublished and a failed page's title (A08-39, A08-40); links inside pages navigate in the store (A08-42); the callout's target (A08-43).
- **API:** an archive or restore that changes nothing leaves the FAQ revision alone (A08-1); a refused banner image is never written (A08-2); the site content reads titles, not bodies (A08-3).
