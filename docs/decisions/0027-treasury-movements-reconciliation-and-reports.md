# 0027 — Treasury movements, nightly reconciliation and reports

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-11 · Amends [0003](0003-money-currencies-and-ledger.md) (new account kinds, the cost of goods signs), [0021](0021-supplier-sync-price-review-and-health.md) (supplier funding in the ledger), [0025](0025-live-operations-reroute-manual-fulfil-dashboard.md) (reports by period)

## Context
F20 asks for a nightly proof that the books are right, and F22 for sales, profit and money reports. The ledger so far records money coming in (deposits) and moving between customers and the store (purchases, refunds, adjustments), but not money leaving the store's places: funding a supplier, the owner withdrawing profit, converting pounds to USDT. Without those, no real balance (a supplier's, the USDT addresses', Sham Cash) can be compared with the books. S08 also posted cost of goods with the opposite signs to the rest of the ledger, so funding and consumption would move a supplier's account the same way. The owner settled the rest in the S13 interview (2026-10-11).

## Decision
- **Treasury movements:** every movement of money between the store's places (Sham Cash SYP and USD, USDT per network, each supplier's prepaid balance) and the outside (the owner and the rest of the world) is recorded from the panel with the amount sent and the amount received; a same-currency difference is a transfer fee, a two-currency movement goes through `currency_exchange` with its implied rate. Supplier balance changes that are not a delivery (refunds after delivery, double charges) are supplier corrections. Re-authentication, a typed confirmation above $100, audit, one reversal (owner, 2026-10-11). New account kinds `outside_funds`, `transfer_fees`, `supplier_corrections`; journal kind `treasury`.
- **Ledger signs:** a posting adds to the account's credit side; money the store holds is negative. Cost of goods posts `cost_of_goods −cost`, `supplier_prepaid +cost`, correcting S08 before any production money exists.
- **Reconciliation:** nightly at 00:30 `Asia/Damascus`, and on demand. Internal checks (journals balance, wallets not negative, orders, attempts, deposits, adjustments and movements against their journals) must match exactly. External checks compare the books with supplier balance reads and on-chain USDT balances automatically, and with Sham Cash balances the admin counts from the app (a reminder after 7 days), within a $1 tolerance (editable) and a range for money in flight; an external difference is rechecked once after 10 minutes (owner, 2026-10-11).
- **Mismatches:** stay open until the admin explains them; an explanation closes that result only, so a lasting difference reopens the next night and the books get fixed by a movement, a correction or an adjustment. Telegram alerts only on a new or changed mismatch, a new warning or a failed run, plus a line in the daily summary. Reconciliation never pauses the store or posts anything (owner, 2026-10-11).
- **Reports:** sales and profit by delivery, as the dashboard (ADR 0025), by day, game, product and supplier; deposits by method; refunds and failure rates; top 50 customers by name and email; and a ledger money-movement report. Real customers only except the ledger report, which shows test customers' share apart. Excel exports carry no phone numbers, player IDs, IPs or codes, and are audited (owner, 2026-10-11).

## Consequences
- Every dollar the store holds has a place in the books, and a recorded movement that did not really happen, a double charge by a supplier or a lying reader shows within a night.
- The admin must record each real movement and an opening balance per place, and count Sham Cash by hand; the panel proposes opening amounts and reminds about counts.
- Reports and the dashboard agree on sales and profit; the ledger report explains the money, and the two meet only over long periods.
