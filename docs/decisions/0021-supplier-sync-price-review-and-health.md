# 0021 — Supplier sync, price review, route tiers and health

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-08 · Amends [0005](0005-supplier-adapters-and-routing.md) (the manual supplier's place in routing, health thresholds, stale costs), [0020](0020-pricing-engine.md) (when a price follows a cost change)

## Context
F09 connects the store to its suppliers: their offers, the mapping of store products to offers, and the costs that prices follow (ADR 0020). The owner had to settle how often costs are refreshed and when a refreshed cost may no longer be trusted, which cost changes wait for the admin instead of moving a price at once, what happens to a price when the cheapest supplier stops, where the manual supplier sits among the routes, the health and balance thresholds, and whether funding a supplier's prepaid balance is recorded now. The supplier accounts and API documentation are not ready yet (Q12), so the real adapters cannot be written with this feature's first PRs.

## Decision
- **Sync:** every supplier with a catalog API is read every 15 minutes, and on demand from the panel. A cost not confirmed by a successful read in the last **2 hours** is unknown: the route is not used and does not set a price. Manual offers carry a cost the admin enters and never go stale (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Price review:** when a sync changes an offer's cost by **more than 10%** either way from the cost the product's current price was built on, and that would change the price, the product keeps its current price and a review is opened (accept the new price, adjust the margin, or pause). Smaller changes reprice at once. While a review is open, the product's price changes only through the review; the margin guard keeps applying to the real, current cost, so a held price that has become unprofitable pauses the product. The threshold and the staleness limit are editable in the panel (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Route changes reprice at once:** when the cheapest usable route changes (a supplier goes down or comes back, runs out of stock, is paused, a mapping changes), the price follows the new cheapest route immediately, up or down, with no review: the store keeps selling while the primary is away and returns to the lower price when it recovers (owner, 2026-10-08). The 10% review applies only to a supplier's own cost changes.
- **Route tiers:** a product's price basis, and the order the router tries routes in (S08), go by tier, then cost: healthy automatic routes, then degraded automatic routes, then the manual route. The manual supplier is a last resort only: it is never preferred over a usable automatic route, whatever its cost (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Health** (A08), from every supplier call in a rolling 30-minute window: `degraded` below 90% success or above a 10-second p90 latency; `down` below 50% success or after 3 consecutive calls with no trustworthy answer; a probe after 10 minutes down. The rates need at least 5 calls in the window. Values editable in the panel; every change of state alerts the admin on Telegram (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Balance** (A07): an alert when a supplier's reported balance falls below its threshold, $50 by default and editable per supplier, repeated every 6 hours while it stays below, and a notice when it recovers (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Import:** products are created from supplier offers in bulk, paused, mapped and priced, and the admin activates them after checking names; the launch catalog is everything the suppliers offer, imported progressively (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Notifications:** one Telegram message after a sync only when something needs the admin (new reviews, products paused by the guard, mapped offers gone), and the open counts in the daily summary (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **No money moves in F09.** Funding a supplier's prepaid balance is recorded with the daily reconciliation (F20, S13); until then the panel shows the balance each supplier reports (owner, 2026-10-08).
- **Adapters after the documentation:** S07 is built and accepted with the `fake` and `manual` suppliers; the `shop2topup` and `wdgzone` adapters each come in their own PR through `/supplier-adapter` once the owner shares the supplier's documentation (Q12). Supplier base URLs live in code, never in the panel.

## Consequences
- A price moves without the admin only for small cost changes and for route changes the admin can see on the supplier page; large moves wait for a decision, and no wait can sell below cost.
- With a single fast-moving supplier down for hours, customers see higher prices from the backup instead of empty shelves; a dead sync shows as "unavailable" after 2 hours instead of selling on old costs.
- The ledger's supplier prepaid accounts stay empty until S08 posts cost of goods and S13 records funding; the reconciliation design must close that gap.
